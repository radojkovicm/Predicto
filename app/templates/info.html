📅 Lock Rule

Predictions close 15 minutes before kickoff. After that, you cannot create or edit a prediction for that match.

Other players' predictions become visible only after a match locks.

🏆 Leaderboard Tie-break

When players are tied on points, the ranking uses:

  1. Total points (higher is better)
  2. Number of correct tips (outcome matched) (higher is better)
  3. Number of exact scores (higher is better)
  4. Time of last prediction (earlier is better — commit to your picks!)
